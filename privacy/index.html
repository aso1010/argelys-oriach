Privacy

What this site collects, and what it does not

Summary

This site collects very little. It has no accounts, no advertising and no cookies of its own.

Analytics

The home page uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and measure page speed. Cloudflare states that it does not use cookies or local storage to collect these measurements, and does not fingerprint visitors. No other page loads analytics.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare receives the address and browser details of each request so that it can send the page.

Fonts, images and films

All of them are served from this site. No third-party font, tracker or embed is loaded.

The intake form

The form on the home page does not send anything to a server. When you press Initialize, it opens your email app with a message addressed to argelys@seveneightythree.com. Nothing is sent until you send it from your email app.

If you send it, I receive your name, your email address, and what you wrote. I use it to reply and for nothing else. I keep it in my email account until I delete it. I do not sell it or share it. To have a message deleted, write to the same address.

Light and dark setting

The story page remembers whether you chose the light or the dark setting. It keeps that choice in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Other sites

Links to LinkedIn, Paragraph, Wild-Lake, Communal Foundation, Seven Eighty Three and CookUnity lead to sites with their own policies.

Questions

Write to argelys@seveneightythree.com. Last updated October 7, 2026. If this page changes, the date changes.
