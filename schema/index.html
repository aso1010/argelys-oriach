# Argelys Samuel Oriach

> Argelys Samuel Oriach is a global macro-systems architect, engineer, writer, artist, activist, and actor with over a dozen years of corporate experience. He is the Nothing Artist and a body and matter architect. He is Founder and Principal Systems Architect of Seven Eighty Three (783), an independent systems design laboratory, Founding Executive Director of Communal Foundation, and Chief Executive Officer of Wild-Lake.

His central deployment is the Universe API, a macro-systems design framework grounded in first-principles engineering and sovereign logic.
