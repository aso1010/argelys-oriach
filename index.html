POSITION

Argelys Samuel Oriach is a global macro-systems architect, engineer, writer, artist, activist, and actor with over a dozen years of corporate experience. As the Nothing Artist, and a body and matter architect, he reverse-engineered his own existence out of systemic poverty from the dirt roads of the Dominican Republic. Now he re-architects digital and organizational infrastructure from the mulch up.

He is Founding Executive Director of Communal Foundation; Founder and Principal Systems Architect of Seven Eighty Three (783), an independent systems design laboratory; and Chief Executive Officer of Wild-Lake, an A&R venture and existential mechanics studio.

His central deployment is the Universe API, a foundational macro-systems design framework grounded in first-principles engineering and sovereign logic.

His short story, This Is Not a Love Story (Yankees), is published at thisisnotalovestory.net.
